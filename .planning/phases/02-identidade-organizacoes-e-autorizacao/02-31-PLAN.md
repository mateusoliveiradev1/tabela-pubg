<interfaces>
Sign-in stores no actor/session/current-method proof.

Link-identity stores server-derived `{ actorId, sessionId }` and requires a pre-existing fresh current-method proof before start.
Link callback produces a pending identity-link proof; final link consumption is handled by the protected management controller in 02-32.
